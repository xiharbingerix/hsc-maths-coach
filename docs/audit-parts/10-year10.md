# Year 10 Core & Advanced — Lesson Catalog Audit

Scope: all `.ts` files under `lib/lessons/year10/` (excluding `index.ts`, which only re-exports).
Every `guidedPractice`, `independentPractice`, `masteryQuiz` (and the equivalent V2 question arrays
consumed by `buildTrigV2Lesson`) was audited on the RESOLVED question, after reading each file's
factory helpers (`algAnswer`, `eqAnswer`, `financeMoney/Number`, `measAnswer`, `numeric/choice`,
`trigAnswer`, `nonRightAnswer`, `probAnswer`, `statAnswer`, `linearAnswer`, `answer`, and their
auto-variant generators). All π/trig/Pythagoras computations were independently recomputed.

Marking model confirmed from `lib/answerMarking.ts`: whitespace, unicode minus, `x²`⇔`x^2`,
decimal⇔fraction, `%`⇔decimal, trailing units (`cm m km $ ° kg …`), leading `$`, thousands
separators, coordinates, ratios, clock times, and a leading `variable =` prefix are all
auto-normalised. Factored/reorderable expressions and compound free-text answers are NOT.

## algebraicTechniques.ts

All expansions, factorisations, DOTS, quadratic factorisations, and algebraic-fraction
simplifications recomputed and correct. MCQ correct options verified; no distractor is also correct.
`algebraicAnswerVariants` correctly supplies ASCII/compact/`var=` forms; fraction answers
(`2x/3`) carry `\frac{...}` accepted forms.

- [P3] algebraic-fractions::alg-fr-m7 — "Simplify 3x/(2x − 6), stating the restriction." bundles two
  distinct deliverables (the simplified expression AND the restriction) into one free-text answer
  `3x/(2(x-3)), x≠3`. Should be `parts`: (a) simplified form, (b) restriction. Evidence: prompt
  explicitly says "stating the restriction" — two deliverables per the Nova multi-part standard.
- [P2] algebraic-fractions::alg-fr-m7 — the compound answer string also rejects reasonable correct
  forms. 3x and 2x−6 share no common factor, so `3x/(2x-6)` is already fully simplified; a student
  typing `3x/(2x-6), x≠3` (denominator left unexpanded) is mathematically correct but not in the
  accepted set, which only lists `2(x-3)` denominators. Current accepted set: `3x/(2(x-3)), x≠3`,
  `3x/[2(x-3)], x≠3`, `…x != 3` variants. Proposed: also accept `3x/(2x-6), x≠3` (and `x != 3`).
