Source: Class Mathematics NSW resources, accessed through Joshua's logged-in Chrome session.

To avoid turning a paid catalogue into a verbatim export, this report focuses on verified counts, course pathways, structural patterns, and curriculum design implications for Nova Maths.

## Structural Pattern

Class Mathematics uses a three-level hierarchy:

1. Course pathway, such as Year 10 Maths Advanced or Year 12 Maths Extension 2.
2. Topic, usually a syllabus strand or assessment-sized unit.
3. Subtopic, usually a single teachable skill, concept, or question family.

The structure is more granular than a traditional textbook chapter list. Most topics break into 5-12 subtopics, and subtopics appear designed to support targeted practice, quick quiz generation, and progress tracking.

## Stage 4 Pattern

Year 7 and Year 8 each use a single Maths pathway. The topic mix is broad and foundational:

- number: integers, whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, rates
- algebra: expressions, equations, substitution, graphing linear equations
- geometry and measurement: angles, area, volume, congruence, Pythagoras
- data and chance: probability, investigating/analysing data

Subtopics are very small skill slices. For Nova, this supports the existing idea of mastery records at subtopic level rather than only unit or lesson level.

## Stage 5 Pattern

Years 9 and 10 are split into Core and Advanced. The Advanced courses are not just longer versions of Core; they introduce additional topics and deeper variants.

Common Core/Advanced areas include algebra, equations, indices, trigonometry, measurement, probability, statistics, and geometry. Advanced adds or expands areas such as quadratics, coordinate geometry, circle geometry, logarithms/polynomials, networks, and non-linear relationships.

The split is useful precedent for Nova's Year 9/10 Core and Advanced separation: keep shared topics aligned where possible, but allow Advanced to have separate topic ordering and extra depth.

## Year 11 Pattern

Class Mathematics keeps both Year 11 (2025) and Year 11 (2026) structures. This is important because the 2026 NSW syllabus transition changes naming and grouping.

The 2026 structure separates:

- Standard: practical money, measurement, data, linear relationships, time/location, networks
- Advanced: algebra, functions, equations/inequations, transformations, coordinate plane, calculus, logs/exponentials, probability/data
- Extension 1: functions, polynomials, trigonometry, permutations/combinations, binomial theorem

Nova should avoid assuming one Year 11 course map is stable across syllabus years.

## Year 12 Pattern

Year 12 is split into five pathways:

- Standard 1: smaller practical pathway, 29 subtopics
- Standard 2: broader practical pathway, 59 subtopics
- Advanced: calculus, functions, finance, statistics, probability, 64 subtopics
- Extension 1: proof, vectors, calculus, projectile motion, binomial distribution, 28 subtopics
- Extension 2: complex numbers, proof, vectors in 3D, integration, mechanics, 40 subtopics

The HSC courses are organised around exam-relevant topic families. The Extension courses are notably compact in topic count but dense in conceptual difficulty.

## Extension 2 Detail

Extension 2 uses five top-level topics:

- Complex Numbers
- Proof
- Further Work With Vectors
- Integration
- Mechanics

The subtopic pattern confirms the important Nova Extension 2 scaffold areas:

- complex arithmetic, Argand geometry, Euler/de Moivre style polar work, polynomial connections
- proof language, proof methods, inequalities, induction
- 3D coordinates, vectors in space, dot product, vector geometry proofs
- advanced integration families including trig, inverse trig, logs, t-formulae, partial fractions
- mechanics including SHM, resisted motion, and projectile motion

This strongly supports adding Argand diagrams and 3D vector diagrams as first-class visual payloads before scaling Extension 2 lessons.

## Design Implications For Nova Maths

- Keep Course > Unit/Topic > Subtopic as the main content backbone.
- Treat subtopics as the core mastery and worksheet targeting unit.
- Preserve Core/Advanced divergence in Years 9 and 10 rather than flattening into one pathway.
- Keep separate Year 11 syllabus-year handling if Nova supports both pre-2026 and 2026 structures.
- For Year 12, prioritise HSC pathway specificity over a generic senior maths structure.
- Extension 2 should remain scaffolded/in-progress until each dense subtopic has authentic lesson, practice, and visual support.
- Worksheet generation should support both topic-level and subtopic-level targeting because Class Mathematics appears built around that workflow.

## Risks And Caveats

- This is a structural audit, not a content-quality audit.
- Exact subtopic labels were used only to verify counts and infer curriculum coverage; this report should not be treated as permission to copy Class Mathematics content.
- Question counts shown on Class Mathematics pages are approximate display counts and were not audited against individual question records.
- Some catalogue cards expose title links inconsistently, so future automated audits should parse visible ordered lists as well as anchors.
