Year 11 & Year 12 Extension 1 Content Strategy
Source: NSW Mathematics Extension 1 11–12 Syllabus (2024)  ·  HSC Maths Coach  ·  Generated 2026-05-26
Implementation context: Year 11 Extension begins new syllabus in 2026. Year 12 Extension 1 begins new syllabus in Term 4 2026. First HSC examination under the new syllabus is 2027. Year 12 lesson wording should not imply this content is "your HSC" until 2027.

Year 11 — Unit 1: Further Work with Functions

  • 1absolute-value-functions — Graphs of y=|f(x)|; solving |f(x)|=a
  • 2absolute-value-inequalities — Solving |f(x)|<a, |f(x)|>a algebraically and graphically
  • 3reciprocal-sqrt-functions — Graphs of 1/f(x) and √f(x); domain/range
  • 4composite-inverse-functions — f∘g, f⁻¹(x); domain restrictions for invertibility
  • 5further-functions-exam-practice — Exam practice across all four concepts

Year 11 — Unit 2: Polynomials

  • 1polynomial-division — Division algorithm P(x)=d(x)Q(x)+R; long division
  • 2remainder-factor-theorem — Remainder theorem; factor theorem; testing factors
  • 3roots-and-coefficients — Vieta's formulas for sum/product of roots (quadratics, cubics)
  • 4graphing-polynomials — Multiplicity of roots; shape near roots; sketching
  • 5polynomials-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 11 — Unit 3: Further Trigonometry

  • 1reciprocal-trig-functions — sec, cosec, cot; graphs; Pythagorean identities
  • 2sum-difference-formulas — sin(A±B), cos(A±B), tan(A±B); exact values
  • 3double-angle-formulas — sin2A, cos2A, tan2A; applications
  • 4t-substitution — t = tan(θ/2); expressing sin/cos/tan in terms of t
  • 5further-trig-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 11 — Unit 4: Permutations and Combinations

  • 1counting-principles — Multiplication principle; tree diagrams; factorial notation
  • 2permutations — Arrangements of n distinct objects; P(n,r) = n!/(n−r)!
  • 3combinations — Selections without order; C(n,r) = n!/(r!(n−r)!)
  • 4arrangements-with-restrictions — At least/at most; identical objects; circular arrangements
  • 5perms-combs-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 11 — Unit 5: The Binomial Theorem

  • 1pascals-triangle — Pascal's triangle; binomial expansion for small n
  • 2binomial-theorem — (a+b)^n = Σ C(n,k) a^(n−k) b^k; full expansion
  • 3general-term — T(k+1) = C(n,k) a^(n−k) b^k; finding a specific term
  • 4binomial-identities — Identities proved by substituting x=1, x=−1 into expansion
  • 5binomial-theorem-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 12 — Unit 1: Proof by Mathematical Induction

  • 1induction-principle — Base case; inductive step; writing formal induction proofs
  • 2induction-summation — Proving summation formulas (1+2+…+n, sum of squares)
  • 3induction-divisibility — Proving divisibility results (e.g. 3^n+2 divisible by 3)
  • 4induction-harder — Inequalities; combinatorial identities; multi-step proofs
  • 5induction-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 12 — Unit 2: Introduction to Vectors

  • 1vectors-intro — Vectors vs scalars; magnitude; geometric representation; notation
  • 2vector-operations — Addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication; triangle/parallelogram law
  • 3components-unit-vectors — Column vector notation; unit vectors î, ĵ; magnitude formula
  • 4dot-product — a·b = |a||b|cosθ; component form; perpendicularity condition
  • 5vectors-geometry — Position vectors; geometric proofs using vectors

Year 12 — Unit 3: Inverse Trigonometric Functions

  • 1inverse-sine — Domain/range of arcsin; graph; evaluating exact values
  • 2inverse-cosine-tangent — arccos, arctan; graphs; domain and range
  • 3inverse-trig-identities — arcsin(x)+arccos(x)=π/2; compositions sin(arccos(x)) etc.
  • 4inverse-trig-equations — Solving equations involving arcsin, arccos, arctan
  • 5inverse-trig-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 12 — Unit 4: Further Calculus Skills

  • 1integration-substitution — u-substitution; indefinite and definite integrals
  • 2inverse-trig-integrals — ∫ 1/√(a²−x²) dx = arcsin(x/a)+C; ∫ a/(a²+x²) dx = arctan(x/a)+C
  • 3further-integration — Harder substitution; combined techniques
  • 4further-calculus-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 12 — Unit 5: Further Applications of Calculus

  • 1related-rates — Implicit differentiation over time; chain rule dA/dt = dA/dr · dr/dt
  • 2volumes-revolution — V = π∫ y² dx; rotating about x-axis or y-axis
  • 3simple-harmonic-motion — ẍ = −n²x; x = a·cos(nt+α); velocity/acceleration relations
  • 4calculus-applications-exam-practice — Exam practice

Year 12 — Unit 6: The Binomial Distribution

  • 1binomial-model — Bernoulli trials; conditions for binomial; X ~ B(n,p) notation
  • 2binomial-probabilities — P(X=k) = C(n,k) p^k (1−p)^(n−k); exact and cumulative
  • 3mean-variance-binomial — E(X)=np; Var(X)=np(1−p); SD(X)
  • 4sampling-distribution-mean — Sample mean X̄; distribution of X̄; standard error
  • 5binomial-distribution-exam-practice — Exam practice

LESSON 1 — Counting Principles and Factorials
slug: "counting-principles"
Topics:
  Multiplication principle: if A can happen m ways and B n ways, total = m×n
  Addition principle: if events are mutually exclusive, total = m+n
  Factorial notation: n! = n × (n−1) × … × 2 × 1
  Simplifying factorial expressions: n!/r!, (n+1)!/n! = n+1
Worked examples (3):
  1. Multiplication principle: 3 shirt colours × 4 trouser colours = 12 outfits
  2. Factorial computation: 6! = 720; 8!/6! = 8×7 = 56
  3. Simplification: (n+2)!/n! = (n+2)(n+1)
Mastery quiz (10): 4 factorials, 3 multiplication principle, 2 addition principle, 1 factorial comparison (MC)

LESSON 2 — Permutations
slug: "permutations"
Topics:
  Ordered arrangements where "order matters"
  Number of ways to arrange n distinct objects in a line: n!
  P(n,r) = n!/(n−r)! — arrange r from n distinct objects
  Computing P(n,r) for given n and r
Worked examples (4):
  1. Arrange 4 books on a shelf: 4! = 24
  2. Choose and arrange 3 runners from 8 for a podium: P(8,3) = 8×7×6 = 336
  3. 3-digit PINs from digits 1–9 (no repetition): P(9,3) = 504
  4. Compute P(10,4) step-by-step: 10×9×8×7 = 5040
Mastery quiz (10): 3 compute P(n,r), 3 contextual arrangements, 2 ordered-vs-unordered MC, 1 formula simplification, 1 comparison MC

LESSON 3 — Combinations
slug: "combinations"
Topics:
  Selections where order does NOT matter
  C(n,r) = n!/(r!(n−r)!) — also written ⁿCᵣ
  Relationship C(n,r) = P(n,r)/r!
  Symmetry property: C(n,r) = C(n,n−r)
  Pascal's identity: C(n+1,r) = C(n,r−1) + C(n,r)
Worked examples (4):
  1. Choose 3 students from 8 for a committee: C(8,3) = 56
  2. Choose 2 toppings from 5: C(5,2) = 10
  3. Use symmetry: C(10,8) = C(10,2) = 45
  4. Calculate C(12,5) step-by-step
Mastery quiz (10): 3 compute C(n,r), 3 contextual selection, 2 symmetry/Pascal, 1 P-vs-C MC, 1 two-stage C×C

LESSON 4 — Arrangements with Restrictions
slug: "arrangements-with-restrictions"
Topics:
  Items together: treat as one unit → (n−1)! × k!
  Items apart: total − together (complement)
  Identical objects: n!/(n₁!·n₂!·…)
  Circular arrangements: (n−1)!
Worked examples (4):
  1. 5 people in a row; A and B must be together: 4! × 2! = 48
  2. A and B must NOT be together: 5! − 48 = 72
  3. Arrange letters of ARRANGEMENT: 12!/(3!·2!·2!·2!)
  4. 6 people at a round table: (6−1)! = 120
Mastery quiz (10): 3 restriction-based line arrangements, 2 circular, 2 identical objects,
                   2 complement method, 1 classify-which-rule MC
